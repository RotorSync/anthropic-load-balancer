OAuth Token Management

Authorize subscriptions to obtain OAuth tokens. Tokens auto-refresh once authorized.

📋 How to Authorize a Subscription ▶
⚠️ Before you start:
Open claude.ai in your browser and sign out of all accounts except the one that matches the subscription you want to authorize.
Steps:
1. Click the Authorize button on the subscription below.
2. An authorization URL will appear. Click Open (or copy the link and paste it into your browser).
3. On the Anthropic page, click Allow to approve the connection.
4. Anthropic will give you an authorization code. Copy the code.
5. Come back here and paste the code into the input field.
6. Click Submit. The status should change to Active.
Tip: Once authorized, tokens auto-refresh — you shouldn't need to do this again unless a token is manually revoked. If you see Auth Failed, re-authorize that subscription.
